## What’s Included in My Services?

### 1. **Domain Registration & Hosting Setup**

The first step in building your website is securing a domain and setting up reliable hosting. The domain is your digital address, and hosting is where your website lives on the internet. I’ll help you choose the perfect domain name that aligns with your brand and is easy for your customers to find.

Once the domain is set up, I’ll take care of the hosting. This includes selecting the right hosting solution based on your business needs, whether it’s shared hosting for a small website or something more robust for larger projects. The setup is seamless, and I’ll ensure everything is configured correctly for performance, security, and scalability.

### 2. **Custom Code Projects**

Your website is not just a static page; it’s a reflection of your business. I offer custom code solutions to ensure your website matches your brand's unique needs. Whether you need a dynamic, interactive experience or a simple, clean layout, I’ll build a website that is both functional and visually appealing.

One of the biggest benefits of working with me is that you will own all the code. I’ll build your website using industry-standard practices, ensuring that the code is clean, efficient, and scalable. This means you can update, modify, or even redesign your website down the line without relying on a third-party service or worrying about code limitations.

### 3. **Up to 5 Pages & Contact Form**

Every business website needs essential pages to convey information to potential customers. I’ll create up to five pages for your site, including core pages such as:

- **Homepage:** Your front door to the internet, showcasing your products, services, and unique selling points.
- **About Page:** A space to tell your brand’s story, values, and mission.
- **Product/Service Pages:** A place to highlight what you offer, with descriptions, pricing, and any other important details.
- **Articles or News Section:** Share updates, articles, or any other content that helps build your authority and connect with customers.
- **Contact Page:** A simple form that makes it easy for customers to get in touch with you, along with your contact information and a map of your location (if needed).

Each of these pages will be crafted to represent your business in the best light, ensuring they’re professional, user-friendly, and optimized for performance.

### 4. **Google Analytics & SEO Integration**

In today’s digital world, having a website isn’t enough—you need to know how your visitors interact with it. That's why I include **Google Analytics** integration in all my projects. Analytics gives you valuable insights into your website’s performance, including visitor traffic, behavior, and conversion rates.

Understanding these metrics allows you to make informed decisions about your website’s content, design, and marketing efforts. It’s an essential tool for growing your business online.

Additionally, I’ll optimize your website for **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**, ensuring it’s visible to search engines like Google. This includes configuring basic on-page SEO, setting up meta tags, optimizing images, and ensuring fast load times. This helps you rank higher in search results, making it easier for potential customers to find your website.
